Community Standards

Server Rules

We keep it simple. Follow these and you'll fit right in.

01

No Griefing

Destroying or tampering with another player's builds is strictly prohibited. Anti-grief plugins are active at all times.

02

Respect the Honor System

Pay for items in shops honestly. We trust you — don't abuse that trust. Theft or bad faith trading will result in a ban.

03

Respect Everyone

Treat all players with respect. Harassment, hate speech, and personal attacks are not tolerated in any form.

04

No Cheating or Hacks

Hacked clients, duplication exploits, and x-ray are banned. Play vanilla-style — it's more satisfying anyway.

05

Keep the World Beautiful

No floating trees, crater holes, or abandoned builds near spawn. Keep the world looking great for everyone.

06

Have Fun

This is a Hermitcraft-inspired server. The spirit is creativity, collaboration, and good vibes. Bring that energy.
